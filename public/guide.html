How your score is calculated

Your score is a single percentage built from all your KPIs. Three steps:

Step 1 — Each KPI becomes a percentage of its target

KPI % = (what you did ÷ your target) × 100

Example: 135 calls against a 150 target = 90%.

Step 2 — Each KPI is capped at 150%

You cannot cover a weak KPI by overdoing another one. Even 300% of target only counts as 150%. This is deliberate — the goal is balanced performance across all KPIs, not one big number.

Step 3 — Weights are applied and everything is added up

Every KPI carries a different weight depending on how much it matters. Multiply each capped percentage by its weight, add them together, and that is your score.

Score = (KPI1% × weight1) + (KPI2% × weight2) + …

Deal override

Closing deals overrides the normal score, because a closed deal is the outcome that actually matters:

A Closer with no calls and no deals always shows a RED CARD, whatever the maths says.

Team health score

A team lead's score is simply the average of their team members' scores — Builders and Closers together, no extra weighting.

Team Health = sum of all member scores ÷ number of members

Example — 6 members

(55 + 112 + 70 + 48 + 58 + 117) ÷ 6 = 77% → YELLOW CARD
Members on leave still count, as 0. If one of those six is on leave for the whole period:

(55 + 112 + 70 + 48 + 58 + 0) ÷ 6 = 57%

Covering for an absent team member is the team lead's responsibility, so the gap shows up in the team score. The same rule is used on the Dashboard, the History page and the TV Board.

Leave & attendance

Leave comes from whatever is marked on the Attendance page. There are two different cases:

Some days off during the period

Your target still covers every working day in the period, including the days you were off. It is not reduced.

5-day week, 1 day leave Calls target 150 × 5 days = 750 (not 600) Minutes target 180 × 5 days = 900 (not 720)
There is no allowance for leave. Taking a day off is your own choice, so the shortfall counts against your score rather than lowering what you were asked to deliver. Your card will show 4/5 days present · Leave: 1 so the reason is visible.

On leave the whole period

  • Your card shows ON LEAVE instead of a score
  • You are not ranked
  • You count as 0 in your team lead's team health score
Attendance drives real numbers, so mark it daily. If someone is not marked as on leave, the system assumes they were working and scores them against a full target either way.

Where the data comes from

Live pages (Dashboard, Combined, Funnel, TV Board)

Read straight from Zoho CRM. Results are cached for 20 minutes, so if you generate the same report twice you get the second one instantly. That means a report can be up to 20 minutes behind — that is normal.

History page

Reads saved snapshots, not live Zoho. A job runs every night and saves that day's numbers permanently. This is why History can show past days instantly, and why today's numbers only appear there tomorrow.

Attendance

Saved directly when you press Save on the Attendance page, and used immediately by every report.

All times use US Eastern business hours, not IST. A "day" runs from midnight to midnight Eastern.

Common questions

My number looks different from yesterday's report for the same day. Why?

Live pages re-read Zoho each time, so if data was still being entered in Zoho when you first ran the report, a later run will differ. The History page never changes once the night's snapshot is saved.

I beat my call target but my score is still low.

Calls are only 20% of a Builder's score, and everything is capped at 150%. Minutes, leads, discoveries and presentations make up the other 80% — check which of those is dragging the score down.

I closed a deal but my card is not gold.

One deal guarantees a Green Card. Two or more guarantee a Golden Card.

Someone is on leave but still shows in the team score.

That is intended — they count as 0. See Team health score.

Who can change the targets?

Team leads can set their own team's monthly targets on the Manage Targets page. Those override the defaults shown here for that team and month.

The TV Board is showing old numbers.

It refreshes on its own every 3 minutes and fully reloads every 4 hours. If it looks stuck, refresh the page with Ctrl+Shift+R.
